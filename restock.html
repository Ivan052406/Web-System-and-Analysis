How these numbers are worked out
  • Avg. daily demand – the 7-day average, leaning on the last 3 days (60% week average + 40% last 3 days).
  • Trend – last 3 days compared with the first 3 days. ▲ rising, ▼ falling, ▶ steady (less than 5% change).
  • Days of stock left – current stock ÷ daily demand.
  • Restock now – stock will run out before a delivery arrives (fewer days left than the delivery time). Restock soon – fewer days left than delivery time + safety stock.
  • Suggested restock – daily demand × (delivery time + stock-up-to days) − current stock.
