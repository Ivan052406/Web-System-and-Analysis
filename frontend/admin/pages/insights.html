How Recommendations Are Calculated

Understand the calculations behind the insights.

01

Average Daily Demand

Total units sold divided by the number of days in the selected analysis period.

02

Demand Trend

Compares average demand in the selected period with the immediately preceding period of equal length.

03

Stock Coverage

Current stock divided by average daily demand estimates how many days inventory may last.

04

Suggested Restock Quantity

Estimates the quantity needed to cover lead time, safety days, and target stock days.
